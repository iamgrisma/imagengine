Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Overview

ImageEngine is a free, privacy-first edge service operated by Grisma. We do not track, profile, sell, or collect personal data from users or automated crawlers.

2. Transient Processing & No Storage

ImageEngine processes incoming SVG content purely in volatile RAM to rasterize pixel buffers. We do not maintain any persistent database of your images, URLs, or metadata.

3. Third-Party CDN Caching

Rendered image binaries are cached at the edge network (Vercel / Cloudflare) to ensure fast content delivery for scrapers and end-users. These caches contain only the public image bytes.

4. No Cookies or Trackers

The ImageEngine API and documentation pages do not set tracking cookies or embed advertising tracking scripts.

5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this policy, contact us via grisma.com.np.
