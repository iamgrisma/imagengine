Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

ImageEngine is a free, privacy-first image generation service operated by Grisma. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we process and — more importantly — what we don't.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, IP address logging)
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No cookies or local storage tokens
  • No advertising trackers or third-party scripts
  • No image content storage or archival

3. Transient Processing

When you submit an SVG for rasterization, it is processed entirely in volatile memory (RAM). The SVG content is parsed, rendered into pixels, and the resulting image is returned to you. After the response is sent, all processing data is discarded. No images, URLs, or metadata are ever written to disk or stored in a database.

4. Edge CDN Caching

Rendered image binaries may be cached by the global edge CDN to ensure fast content delivery. These caches contain only the public image bytes and are automatically purged based on cache TTL policies. ImageEngine does not control or access individual cached entries.

5. Server Logs

Standard server infrastructure may produce transient access logs (HTTP method, status code, response time) for operational health monitoring. These logs do not contain request payloads, image content, or personally identifiable information, and are automatically rotated and deleted.

6. Third-Party Services

ImageEngine uses a global edge content delivery network for performance. No third-party analytics, advertising, or user tracking services are integrated into the application.

7. Children's Privacy

ImageEngine does not knowingly collect any information from children. The service is a developer-facing API with no user accounts or registration.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions regarding this policy, contact us via grisma.com.np.
